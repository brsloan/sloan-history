The Family Tree

people from Ben’s research tree on Ancestry, as of September 2026. The chart shows five generations at a time, reading left to right from child to ancestors. Click a name for details; double-click (or use the links in the panel) to re-draw the chart from that person. A red “›” means there are more ancestors further back.

Blue edge = male, rose edge = female. Living people are left out of this tree, except those in the direct line, who appear by first name only. The tree was exported from Ancestry and carries that site’s usual hazards: lines beyond about 1800 that aren’t discussed in the story pages should be treated as leads, not facts. The GEDCOM files themselves are in Genealogy / Trees, and printable pedigree charts are here: Harold Sloan pedigree (PDF) · Ellis Sloan pedigree (PDF) · Ben’s full pedigree (PDF).
